## 📡 Where to apply — channels

1. **Company career portals (primary)** — set alerts for "Platform Engineer", "DevOps Engineer", "SRE", "Site Reliability", "Cloud Engineer".
2. **LinkedIn (setup)** — headline: `Platform/DevOps Engineer — K8s · Terraform · AWS · CI/CD`; turn on "Open to Work" (recruiters only); follow targets; apply via posts + referral asks.
3. **Naukri + Instahyre + Cutshort** — India-specific boards; Instahyre/Cutshort are product-company-heavy.
4. **Referrals (highest conversion)** — find 1-2 engineers at each target (LinkedIn), send a short note with your portfolio link. Referrals convert 5-10x cold applies.
5. **Remote-first boards** — remoteok.com, weworkremotely.com, workatastartup.com (Wellfound).
6. **Meetups & conferences** — Pune DevOps meetups, CNCF meetups, Docker/K8s community days (~30% of Pune DevOps hires come from referrals/networking per research).

## 🗺 4-week application playbook

- **Week 1:** Pick 10 Tier-1+2 companies. Set career-page alerts; polish LinkedIn + resume to match their exact JD keywords (K8s, Terraform, CI/CD, SRE).
- **Week 2:** Apply to 5 Tier-2 (volume) companies first — fast interview reps + a safety offer. Treat them as practice.
- **Week 3:** After 2-3 interview reps, apply to Tier-1 product companies with a referral. Attach A06 capstone + B05 GitOps repo links.
- **Week 4+:** Tier-3 stretch (Datadog/Grafana/HashiCorp/Cloudflare) after portfolio + mocks are tight. Target **15-20 active applications**, not 100 spam ones.
- Track every application in a sheet: company, role, date, stage, next action. Follow up after 7 days of no response.
- Certifications (CKA, AWS DevOps Associate) measurably boost Tier-2 shortlisting (~40% per Pune 2025 research) — schedule during B01/B03.
